About me

01 / INTRODUCTION

I build learning-based systems
for humanoid robots.

I am an Artificial Intelligence graduate of South China Agricultural University (SCAU). From July 2025 to September 2026, I worked as a Robotics Algorithm Researcher at CLAI-LAB, connecting policy learning and simulation with physical robot deployment.

My work focuses on perceptive locomotion, whole-body control, and motion imitation. I am especially interested in how robots can use motion history, future-state prediction, and multimodal perception to navigate complex environments.

I have contributed to humanoid parkour research, martial-arts motion development, and reusable sim-to-real pipelines, alongside earlier work in efficient computer vision.
